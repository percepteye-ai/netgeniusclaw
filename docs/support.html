NetGeniusClaw Mobile — Support

NetGeniusClaw Mobile is the companion app for a self-hosted NetGeniusClaw Border. This page is where to go if you have a question, hit a problem, or want to request a feature.

Before you ask

NetGeniusClaw Mobile does not work on its own — it requires a NetGeniusClaw Border you (or your operator) already run. If the app is stuck on the "scan an enrollment QR code" screen, that's expected until a Border is set up and you scan its enrollment code.

Report a problem or ask a question

Support is provided through the project's GitHub repository. Open an issue describing what happened, what you expected instead, and your iOS version and app version (Settings screen, or General → About on your Border) — this is the fastest way to get help:
